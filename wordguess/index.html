Key features

  • Guess words letter by letter across 48 kid-safe categories.

Frequently asked questions

What is free?

Six categories are free in each language.

What does Brain Trainer Plus add?

All 48 categories, five daily Jokers and the daily archive. Monthly, yearly and Lifetime options are available; the listing describes a seven-day trial on the yearly plan.
