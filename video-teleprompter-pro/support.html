Need help?

Open an issue at github.com/yohann305/app-legal/issues and mention “Video Teleprompter”. Please include your device model and iOS version, and do not include passwords or payment details.

Common questions

How do I restore Pro or my lifetime purchase? Open the App → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Earlier lifetime and monthly purchases are recognized automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription? iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Video Teleprompter Pro, or App → Settings → Manage subscription.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my videos? Every take is kept in the App under Takes for that script, and is also saved to your Photos library when Photos access is allowed. If takes are not appearing in Photos, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Video Teleprompter Pro.

The script doesn't follow my voice. Voice-scroll needs Microphone and Speech Recognition permission, and works best when the script's language matches your device language. You can always switch back to steady scrolling.

How do I use another iPhone or iPad as a remote? Install the App on both devices, keep them on the same Wi-Fi or with Bluetooth on, allow Local Network access, then open Remote on the second device and pick the recording device.

Can I record in landscape? Yes. Rotate your device before recording; the video follows the orientation.
