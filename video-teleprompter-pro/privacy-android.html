Privacy Policy

Video Teleprompter Pro (Android)
Operator: Yohann Taieb
Effective date: November 8, 2026

At a glance

  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your scripts, settings and recorded videos stay on your device.
  • Voice scroll and captions use Android's speech recognition. On some devices, that service may process audio on Google's servers.
  • The remote control sends commands over your local Wi-Fi network, without our servers.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Yohann Taieb (“we,” “us”) handles information for the Android app Video Teleprompter Pro (the “App”), published on Google Play.

2. Information we collect

None. The App has no backend server, no user accounts, and no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not receive your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, scripts, recordings, or usage data.

3. Scripts, settings and videos on your device

The scripts you write or import (text, Markdown, RTF, HTML, PDF, Word files), your folders, prompter settings, and the takes you record are stored locally in the App's private storage on your device. When you save a take to your gallery, it is written to the Movies folder under "Video Teleprompter Pro" using Android's MediaStore. Files you import are read only after you pick them with the system file picker. Uninstalling the App deletes its private data. Videos you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them. Your device's backups (for example Google's device backup) may include App data, depending on your settings.

4. Camera and microphone

The App uses the camera and microphone only to record you while you read, and only after you grant permission in Android. Recordings are not uploaded anywhere by the App.

5. Speech recognition

Voice scroll (the script follows your speech) and captions use Android's SpeechRecognizer. The App asks for on-device recognition when available. If your device does not have an offline speech model for the language you use, Android's speech service may send the audio to Google's servers under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive that audio or the transcript. Voice scroll and captions are optional and only run when you turn them on.

6. Remote control (local network)

The remote-control feature finds other devices running the App on your Wi-Fi network (Android Network Service Discovery) and connects to them directly. Between your devices, the App exchanges playback commands (play, pause, speed, next script) and status: playback progress and speed, whether a take is recording, the title of the current script, the titles of the scripts in the set list, and the device name shown when pairing. Nothing passes through our servers. Because the connection runs on your local network without encryption, use the remote only on a Wi-Fi network you trust. Android may ask for the "Nearby devices" permission for this feature; the App does not use it to determine your location.

7. In-app purchases

The Pro and Studio subscriptions, Lifetime Pro, and add-on packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and manages subscriptions under Google's Privacy Policy. The App only receives confirmation of what you are entitled to; we do not receive your payment information.

8. Sharing

When you share a take or caption file, it is handed to the Android share sheet and nothing is sent unless you choose a destination. The App may show Google Play's in-app review prompt; that prompt is handled by Google Play.

9. Children

Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are subject to the controls of your Google Play account.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on a server. Local data is fully under your control on your device. We do not sell or share personal information.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
