30-Day On-Camera Challenge

January 4 – February 1, 2027

Build on-camera confidence in 30 days: record one short video a day while reading your script right under the lens with Video Teleprompter Pro.

How to take part: write a short script (30–60 seconds), place your phone at eye level, set a comfortable scroll speed and record. Post it or keep it private — the habit is what counts.

Ideas: a tip from your work, a product demo, a story, a weekly update, or a short talk you want to rehearse.

Free to join. No purchase is required to take part.
