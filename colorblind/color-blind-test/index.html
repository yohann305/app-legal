How this sample test works

Each plate is a circle of colored dots. The number is drawn with dots whose hue differs from the background mostly along the red–green axis, while brightness is kept similar. People with typical color vision see the number easily; people with a red–green color vision difference may see a different number or none at all. The first plate is a control that almost everyone can read. Plates are generated fresh each time, so you can retake the test.

FAQ

Is an online color blind test accurate?

It is a screening sample. Displays vary a lot, so a missed plate here is a reason to take a proper test, not a conclusion.

What is the most common type of color blindness?

Red–green deficiency. It affects roughly 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women of Northern European ancestry; it is usually inherited and present from birth.

Can I use this for a job or a driving license?

No. Employers, transport authorities and licensing bodies set their own requirements and usually require an in-person exam. Use this page and the app to prepare for it.

Is this the Ishihara test?

No. These are original pseudo-isochromatic plates generated in your browser in the same general style. Ishihara is a trademark of its respective owner.
