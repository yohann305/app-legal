New Year Color Vision Check

January 4 – 30, 2027

Start the year with a quick check of your color vision. The free 14-plate Quick Screen in Colorblind Eye Exam Test takes about two minutes on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

How to take part: open the app, choose Quick Screen, read the number in each plate, and see your screening result. You can retake it any time, and try the full 38-plate test for a closer look.

Tips: turn off Night Shift and True Tone, use normal room light and hold the device at arm’s length.

This is a screening, not a diagnosis. Only an eye care professional can diagnose a color vision deficiency.
