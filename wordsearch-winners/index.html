Cube

Spin it. Words cross the faces, including the seams where two sides meet. A word search you can turn in your hand.

Pyramid

Same hunt, steeper sides. The faces climb to a point, and the words climb with them while the shape turns.

Globe

Letters on a world you can twirl. Find the word before it rolls out of sight.
