WordSearch Winners

A word search with giant boards and rotating 3D puzzles.

For: Word-puzzle players who enjoy daily challenges and large hunts.

Key features

  • Spin cube, globe and pyramid puzzles.
  • Explore All Words 2026 with 92,064 English words on one board.
  • Play daily and year challenges, with offline solo play and no ads.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a leaderboard rank?

No. Hint spotlights reveal a word, but that word scores half. Ranks are not sold.

What purchases are offered?

Winners Club, hint packs, themes and other optional packs. Check the app for current contents and prices.
