Privacy Policy

WordSearch Winners · Last updated September 19, 2026

WordSearch Winners is an open-source word-search game operated by Yohann Taieb.

Information you provide

You can play without an account. If you create an optional account, we process the account identifier and credentials needed to authenticate you. Scores, achievements, challenge results, and leaderboard entries may be associated with your account when you use those features.

Gameplay and technical data

The app processes gameplay actions and results to provide puzzles, challenges, achievements, and leaderboards. Hosting infrastructure may receive basic request data such as an IP address and timestamp to operate and secure the service.

Ads, tracking, and sharing

There are no ads. We do not sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers. Information is shared only as needed to provide requested features, comply with law, protect the service, or at your direction.

Storage and choices

Optional account and game data may be stored by the app's PocketBase backend or hosting provider. You may request access, correction, or deletion of account information, or stop using account features. Do not put sensitive information in public usernames, scores, or issue reports.

Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the app changes; the date above shows the latest revision.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, use the project issue tracker: github.com/yohann305/wordsearch-international/issues.
