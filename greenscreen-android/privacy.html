Privacy Policy

Green Screen Live (Android)
Operator: Yohann Taieb
Draft · Effective date: November 10, 2026

Draft. This Privacy Policy is a working draft for the Green Screen Live Android app on Google Play. It is adapted from the iOS policy and checked against the Android app’s current permissions, sign-in options, and third-party SDKs. It is not legal advice and may be updated.

At a glance

  • Green Screen Live is an Android app for recording chroma-key (green screen) video.
  • Camera, microphone, and photos/videos access are used to record, import, and save video.
  • Social accounts and a feed are optional. Sign-in may use email, Google, or Facebook through Firebase Authentication.
  • The App may show ads (Google Mobile Ads), offer in-app purchases or subscriptions through Google Play Billing, and send push notifications (OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Firebase Analytics collection is turned off. Usage events and crash reports are stored in the App’s private files on your device and are not uploaded by that on-device logger.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Yohann Taieb (“we,” “us,” or “the operator”) handles information in connection with the Android app Green Screen Live (the “App”), published on Google Play.

The App is an application for recording video with a chroma-key (green screen) effect. You can replace or composite a background while recording, using the camera, microphone, and photos or videos you choose from your library.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers information processed in connection with the Android App. A separate policy applies to the iOS version of Green Screen Live.

It does not apply to third-party websites or services that we do not control, including Google, OneSignal, Klipy, Facebook, and GitHub, when you use those services under their own terms.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Camera, microphone, and media library

The App requests these Android permissions only to provide recording and media features:

  • Camera — to capture video for green-screen recording.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your video.
  • Photos and videos (including, on Android 12 and earlier, access to write to shared storage) — to import a photo or video you select and to save recordings you choose to keep.

You can refuse or revoke these permissions in Android Settings. Features that need a permission will not work without it. Camera, microphone, and library content stay on your device unless you choose to export, share, or post them.

3.2 Optional accounts and feed

You can record without creating an account. If you choose to sign in, the App may offer optional social accounts and a feed. Sign-in is provided with Firebase Authentication and may include email and password, Google, or Facebook. Feed and profile data may be stored with Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Storage.

If you use those features, we or the account service may process:

  • Account identifiers and sign-in details the account service provides (for example, an email address, a display name, or a social sign-in identifier).
  • Profile information you enter, such as a name or bio.
  • Content you post to the feed, and related engagement such as follows.

3.3 In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google through Google Play Billing. Google receives your payment details under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your full payment card number.

We may receive purchase status from Google Play, such as a product identifier, whether a purchase or subscription is active, and a purchase or account token, so the App can unlock the feature you bought. You can manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

3.4 Advertising

The App includes Google Mobile Ads and may display advertisements. Whether ads are shown can be controlled by a remote configuration flag. When ads are shown, Google may process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, the Android advertising ID, if you have not opted out of personalized ads).
  • Approximate technical data used to deliver and measure ads, such as IP address, device type, and app version.

That processing is described in Google’s policies. On Android you can reset or opt out of personalized ads in Google settings (for example, Ads or Privacy settings on the device). We do not sell your personal data.

3.5 Push notifications

The App uses OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging to send push notifications. If you allow notifications, OneSignal, Google, and the App may process:

  • A OneSignal or similar device or user identifier, and a Firebase Cloud Messaging token.
  • The fact that notifications are enabled on the device.
  • Language, time zone, or similar technical details used to deliver messages.

You can turn notifications off in Android Settings at any time. Notification delivery is not guaranteed.

3.6 On-device diagnostics

Firebase Analytics collection is turned off in the App. The App may still write usage events (for example, that a paywall was shown or a purchase was restored) and crash reports to the App’s private files on your device. Those local records include technical details such as the App version, not your name or payment card number. The on-device logger that writes these events does not send them over the network. Uninstalling the App removes this local data, subject to any device backup you have enabled.

3.7 Other network features

When you use optional backgrounds, overlays, or GIF stickers provided by Klipy, the App requests that content from Klipy’s service. Klipy may receive basic request data such as an IP address and user-agent in order to return the media you asked for.

The App may also fetch remote configuration flags from this GitHub Pages site so we can control features such as the launch paywall. That request is an ordinary web request and is not used to build an advertising profile.

The App may show Google Play’s in-app review prompt; that prompt is handled by Google Play.

3.8 Technical data

When the App contacts a service that supports a feature you use (accounts, feed, ads, notifications, purchases, configuration, or sticker/background content), that service may receive basic request data such as an IP address, user-agent, app version, and timestamp. We use this to operate, secure, and diagnose those features.

The App also uses standard Android permissions for internet access, network and Wi‑Fi state, a wake lock while recording or processing, and billing with Google Play.

3.9 Device backup

The App allows Android backup. If you use your device’s backup service (for example, Google Backup), App data on the device may be included in that backup under the backup provider’s terms.

3.10 Contact and support

If you contact us, we receive the information you include in that message (for example, a GitHub username and the text of an issue). GitHub processes that information under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide chroma-key video recording, import, and saving.
  • Operate optional accounts and the feed when you use them.
  • Process and restore in-app purchases and subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Show ads through Google Mobile Ads, when ads are enabled.
  • Send push notifications you have allowed, through OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Load optional sticker or background content you request from Klipy.
  • Keep local diagnostic records on the device, operate, maintain, secure, and debug features that are enabled.
  • Respond to questions, issues, and requests.
  • Comply with law where applicable.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties for their own independent marketing lists. Advertising shown in the App is served by Google Mobile Ads as described above; that is not a sale of personal data by us.

5. How we share information

We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers for features you use. Optional accounts and the feed may be stored with Firebase. Ads are served by Google. Push notifications are delivered with OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Optional sticker and background content may be fetched from Klipy. Each provider processes data under its own terms and only as needed for that feature.
  • Other users. Content and profile details you post to the feed may be visible to other users of that feature.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect users, the App, or others.
  • Business transfer. If operation of the App is transferred, information may move with it, still subject to this policy or a successor notice.

6. Retention

Camera, microphone, and media-library content stays on your device until you delete it, except for copies you choose to export, share, or post. Feed and account data are kept while your account is active and as needed to display the feed.

Purchase records may be kept as long as needed to honor a purchase or subscription and to meet accounting or legal duties. Push tokens are kept while notifications are enabled and are no longer used for delivery after you disable them or uninstall the App, subject to the provider’s own retention.

On-device diagnostic and crash files stay in the App’s private storage until they are rotated, you clear the App’s data, or you uninstall the App.

If you ask us to delete an account, we will delete or de-identify associated personal data we control, unless we must keep a limited record (for example, to resolve a dispute, prevent abuse, or comply with law). Copies you already posted may persist in other users’ caches or exports for a time we do not control.

7. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the features in use, including relying on the access controls of the account, notification, and purchase services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Media on your device is protected by your device lock and Android security.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. Recording does not require an account. If you create an account, use the feed, or make a purchase, you must be old enough to do so under the laws of your country, or you must have a parent or guardian’s consent where that is required.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) without required parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

Do not put sensitive personal information in public usernames, feed posts, or public GitHub issues.

9. Your choices and rights

You may:

  • Deny or revoke camera, microphone, notifications, and photos/videos access in Android Settings.
  • Use recording features without creating an account.
  • Decline optional accounts, the feed, and push notifications.
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your Google or device ads settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play (Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of account information by contacting us.
  • Uninstall the App or clear the App’s data to remove local data stored on your device, including on-device diagnostic files (this does not cancel a Google Play subscription or delete a feed account by itself).

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights. We do not sell personal information as that term is commonly used in U.S. state privacy laws (including the “Do Not Sell or Share” rights under the CCPA/CPRA). Advertising in the App is provided by Google Mobile Ads; you can use Google’s ads controls to limit personalized ads.

10. International processing

The operator and the service providers named in this policy (including Google, OneSignal, Klipy, Facebook if you use Facebook sign-in, and Firebase account or feed services) may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. If you use those features, your information may be processed in those locations.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you should review the revised policy. If changes are material, we will provide a more prominent notice where reasonable (for example, in the App or on this page).
