Open an issue at github.com/yohann305/greenscreen_ios/issues. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive credentials in a public issue. If your request involves personal data, say so in the issue.

Common questions

Do I need a green backdrop? Chroma key removes a selected color. An evenly lit solid-color backdrop works best; this is not a promise of AI subject removal in every environment.

Is everything free? No. The app is free to download and includes paid subscriptions and in-app purchases. Check the purchase screen for the features, local price, period and any trial terms.
