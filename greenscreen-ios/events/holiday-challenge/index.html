Holiday Green Screen Challenge

December 14, 2026 – January 3, 2027

Record a holiday video in front of snow, city lights or any place you dream of, using live chroma key in Green Screen Live on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

How to take part: hang a green (or blue) cloth, open the app, pick a festive photo or video background, tap the backdrop color to remove it, and record. Share your video wherever you like.

Tips: light the backdrop evenly, stand 1–2 m in front of it and avoid wearing green.

Free to join. No purchase is required to take part.
