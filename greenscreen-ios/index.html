Frequently asked questions

Do I need a green backdrop?

Chroma key removes a selected color. An evenly lit solid-color backdrop works best; this is not a promise of AI subject removal in every environment.

Is everything free?

No. The app is free to download and includes paid subscriptions and in-app purchases. Check the purchase screen for the features, local price, period and any trial terms.

Choosing a green screen app for iPhone

Compare live chroma-key recording, timeline editing and AI background removal. Green Screen Live Video Record lists live chroma-key recording on iPhone and iPad. Chroma key replaces a selected color; it is not the same as AI subject segmentation. Use a suitable backdrop and even lighting.

Does downloading it unlock every feature?

No. It is free to download; paid features and the locally displayed purchase terms apply.
