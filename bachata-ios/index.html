Bachata Dance App

Learn sensual bachata through instructor-led video lessons.

For: Beginners and social dancers practising at their own pace.

App Store

Key features

  • Watch step-by-step lessons from instructors including Sofia and Kelvin.
  • Save moves as Favorite, Practicing or Learned.
  • Browse dance events; the app supports English, French and Spanish.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks every course and lesson. Weekly, monthly and annual plans are listed in the app.
