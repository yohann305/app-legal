Privacy Policy

Bachata Dance App
Operator: Yohann Taieb
Effective date: October 10, 2026

At a glance

  • Bachata Dance App is an iOS app for learning bachata dance moves through video lessons.
  • You can browse lessons without an account. An account (email and password) is needed to post videos, mark favorites, or subscribe.
  • Videos you upload are hosted by Vimeo; lesson videos are streamed from Vimeo.
  • Camera, microphone, and photo library access are used only to record or pick videos you choose to upload.
  • Location (while using the app) can be used to show nearby dance events.
  • In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not see your card number.
  • No advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics are embedded in the App.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Yohann Taieb (“we,” “us,” or “the operator”) handles information in connection with Bachata Dance App (the “App”), an iOS application for learning bachata dance moves through video lessons, event listings, and a community video feed.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the App and the services we operate for it: the account system, the community feed, event listings, and in-app purchases. It does not apply to third-party services we do not control, such as Vimeo, Apple, or linked websites, which operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

3. Information we collect

We collect what is needed to operate the features you use. If you do not use a feature, we do not need the information that feature requires.

3.1 Account information

Browsing lessons works without an account. If you register, we collect:

  • Email address and password (the password is stored in hashed form on our server).
  • Display name and optional profile photo you choose to provide.
  • A session token used to keep you signed in. The token is stored securely in your device’s Keychain.

Your display name and profile photo can be visible to other users in the feed when you post.

3.2 Content you upload

If you record or upload a dance video, we use:

  • Camera and microphone — to record video with audio, only while you are actively recording.
  • Photo library — to let you pick an existing video or photo to upload, and to save files you choose.

Videos you upload are stored and streamed through Vimeo, which hosts the video files and serves them to viewers. Vimeo processes the video data under its own privacy policy. We store references to your uploads (title, thumbnail, playback links) on our server so they can appear in your profile and the feed.

3.3 Preferences and lists

When signed in, we store your favorites, practice list, learned-move list, and lesson progress so they sync across your devices.

3.4 Location

The App can request location while in use to show bachata events and dancers near you. You can refuse or revoke this permission in iOS Settings; the events list still works when you pick a location manually.

3.5 Feedback

If you send feedback through the App, we receive the message text you write and your account identifier so we can reply.

3.6 Information we do not collect

The App does not embed advertising SDKs or third-party analytics trackers. We do not sell your personal data.

4. How we use information

  • To operate your account, the feed, favorites, and lists.
  • To host, transcode, and stream videos you upload (via Vimeo).
  • To show nearby events when you enable location.
  • To process subscriptions through Apple and unlock paid content.
  • To respond to feedback and keep the service working and secure.

5. Who we share with

  • Vimeo — hosts and streams the videos you upload and the lesson library.
  • Apple — App Store account, in-app purchase processing, and (if you rate the App) StoreKit rating prompts.
  • Our hosting providers run the App’s server and storage.

We do not share your personal data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. You can delete your account from within the App (Account settings). Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, and uploaded videos from the App. Cached copies (for example, CDN video caches) expire on their own schedules.

7. Security

The App talks to our server over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed, and your session token is kept in the device Keychain. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your credentials private.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us using the support link below and we will handle the request consistent with applicable law.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
